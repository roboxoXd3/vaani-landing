The science

Speech isn't slower thinking. It's thinking, unfiltered.

Typing forces your brain through an extra translation step — form the thought, then fire off one discrete keystroke at a time. Speech skips that step entirely: your language centers produce words as a continuous stream, the way they evolved to.
