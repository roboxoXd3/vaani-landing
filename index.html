The science

Speech isn't slower thinking. It's thinking, unfiltered.

Every session note and client email you type forces your brain through an extra step — form the thought, then peck it out one keystroke at a time. Speech skips that step entirely: you already think in full sentences, so just say them.

For illustration: 15 minutes of write-up after each of 10 weekly sessions is roughly 2.5 hours of typing. At the ratio above, saying the same thing takes well under an hour — about two hours back in your week, every week.

Factor in backspacing, typo correction, and the mental effort of translating thought into keystrokes — and the real-world gap widens to nearly 5×.
